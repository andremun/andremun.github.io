## Research and Industry Engagement

My research interests are randomised optimisation heuristics, automated optimisation and machine learning, operations research, experimental algorithmics, and modelling and simulation.

- **45 journal articles** and **30 conference papers**; **2,385 citations** ([Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qIw0wzkAAAAJ&hl=en), 1,397 in Scopus), **h-index 25** (19 in Scopus), with 1,651 (1,027) citations over the last five years on an upward trajectory
- Invited participant at Schloss Dagstuhl seminars (2016, 2023) and Lorentz Centre workshop *Benchmarked* (2020)
- Tutorial presenter at GECCO'24, PPSN'24, IJCNN'24, PRICAI'23, CEC'20
- Lead developer of [MATILDA](https://matilda.unimelb.edu.au/matilda/) (Melbourne Algorithm Test Instance Library with Data Analytics), a cloud platform for Instance Space Analysis with 200+ users across 4+ countries and 3,000+ jobs processed
- Industry partners, as researcher or supervisor of student teams:
  - **Future Fibre Technologies Ltd.** (2016–2019) — methods for detecting intrusion events across various operating environments while mitigating nuisances
  - **Powerline Bushfire Safety Program** (2017) — algorithms to identify plant species likely to cause a fault on a 22 kV power line
  - **Boeing Australia** (2021–2024) — methodologies using multi-fidelity information to model an aircraft flight envelope
  - **IntelliSchool** (2022) — models for identifying groups of students based on academic development
  - **HatchQuarter** (2022) — a model to predict demand for milk derivatives in global markets
  - **AGL Ltd.** (2024) — an anomaly detection model for predictive maintenance on wind turbines
